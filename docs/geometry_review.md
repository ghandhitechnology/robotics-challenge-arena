# Geometry review

The revised scene was reviewed against the full Korean guide and the official international supplement. National dimensions take priority. The active field is 1143×1181 mm; the international 500 mm H bars, 400 mm gap, cylinder spacing, 60 mm holes and 20 mm-high beams replace earlier image estimates. The plate retains its national 345 mm length and now has 18 mm clearance to either adjacent tape.

Both the primary review and an independent reviewer inspected the final top, overview, asset-detail, framed, senior and source-comparison renders. The photo scenes contain twelve cylinders, ten kits, three samples and two physical beams. The senior scene contains twelve cylinders, four kits and three samples. Colors, beam ordering, upward emblems, holes and revised wall height match the final specification. No concrete visual defect was found.

Vertex measurements and collision checks cover all physical parts. The frame uses a 20×65 mm international perimeter section with rail lengths adapted to one national field. Every fence-free Blender scene has its frame objects removed, so hidden rigid bodies cannot form an invisible boundary. Senior omissions remove the associated bodies and graphics as well.

The international dimensions take precedence over distorted raster positions. The resulting source-image centroid differences and all derived dimensions are reported in [verification.md](../output/verification.md). Lab-hole pitch, undimensioned object placement, emblem sizing, support thickness and material physics remain documented assumptions. Isaac Sim runtime testing remains for an NVIDIA GPU machine.
