# International dimension audit

The [official Korean resource page](https://robotics-2026.web.app/resources) links the [ITU Robotics for Good Youth Challenge 2026-2027 Health Emergencies rulebook](https://robotics-2026.web.app/files/intl-rulebook-en.pdf). The downloaded PDF has 24 pages and a creation date of 20 July 2026. Relevant figures and dimension arrows were inspected directly. The [ITU challenge page](https://aiforgood.itu.int/robotics-for-good-youth-challenge/) confirms the current theme and edition. Research date: 9 September 2026.

All measurements below are millimetres. Page references use the printed international rulebook page numbers.

| Item | Explicit international dimensions | Application to this model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single competition field, p7 and p13 | 1181 ± 6 by 1143 ± 5 | Preserve the national photo orientation: X 1143 by Y 1181. |

Use the 571.5 top datum against national Y 1181: horizontal connector Y 609.5; vertical bars Y 359.5 to 859.5. |

| Medical kits, p10 | 25 by 25 by 20; cross 20 by 20 with 5 mm wide strokes; ten in international kit | Photo setup retains ten. National preliminary setup follows its separate kit quantities. |
| Sample, p9 | Diameter 56 and thickness 5 | Shared by both setups. |

The H coordinates above combine the international local dimensions with the national outer labels. They retain the 571.5 top offset rather than centring the marking at Y 590.5. The schematic rotates relative to the photograph without consistently rotating its outer labels; it does not resolve every field dimension independently. In particular, the healthcare length chain gives 503 for the middle clear span, 300 for the right clear span and two 20 mm wide divisions; the left clear span has no explicit dimension. Treating both outer clear spans as 300 would total 1143, which conflicts with the photo's 1181 axis after rotation.

The national document governs the preliminary map, plate length and kit contents. The international figures supply missing dimensions for the central marking, patient grid, laboratory holes and full photo setup beams. The framed photo setup adopts the international perimeter long-wall section of 20 mm thickness and 65 mm height on all four rails. Rail lengths follow the national single-field dimensions. This is a single-field adaptation; the international shared divider has a different 19 by 70 mm section and is not included.

Emblem size and placement on beams are visual estimates. Their 31 mm wide top emblem reuses the sample's vector biohazard mesh. The wooden body bounds follow the explicit dimensions without bevels.

No native CAD was found during the targeted official search. The Korean resource listing marks appendix B1-B4 files as awaiting distribution, and the international PDF contains no embedded files. The unchanged official PDF is included as `reference/challenge_2026_international.pdf`.
